## How It Works

The glass effect combines:
- **Semi-transparent backgrounds** (rgba colors with 0.7-0.9 opacity)
- **Backdrop blur** (12-20px blur for depth)
- **Subtle borders** (white with low opacity)
- **Inner highlights** (inset shadows for shine)
- **Smooth shadows** (multiple layers for depth)

## Technical Details

All glass effects use:
- `backdrop-filter: blur()` for frosted glass effect
- `-webkit-backdrop-filter` for Safari compatibility
- `rgba()` colors for transparency control
- `inset box-shadow` for inner glow
- Smooth transitions for interactive states

The effects are performance-optimized and work across all modern browsers.
